# Inviting team members

Invitations are scoped to a single organization, so a new teammate sees only the workspace you invited them to.

## Who can invite

Admins and owners. If you cannot see the invite control, you are a member or a viewer — ask an admin.

## Sending an invitation

Go to **Settings → Team members** and choose **Invite**. Enter the email address and pick the role you want them to land in.

The role you pick here is the role they get. It is not a temporary trial role — if you are unsure, invite as **Member** and promote later. Only owners can change roles, so getting it right up front avoids a second step.

## What happens next

The invited person receives an email with a link to accept. Accepting creates their account if they do not have one, then attaches them to this organization with the role you selected.

Until they accept, the invitation sits in the team list as pending. The address is not a member yet and consumes nothing.

## Resending

If the email does not arrive, resend it from the same row in the team list. The previous link stops working and a fresh one is issued.

Check spam first — the message comes from your own deployment's sending domain, which a fresh install may not have verified yet.

## Revoking

Pending invitations can be revoked at any time by an admin or owner. Revoking cancels the link immediately; nobody can accept it afterwards.

To remove someone who already accepted, see [Managing members](/docs/organizations/managing-members).
