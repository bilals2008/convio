# Transferring ownership

Every organization has exactly one owner, and the owner is the only role that can change hands this way.

## Before you transfer

Ownership controls three irreversible things: deleting the organization, wiping all data, and transferring ownership again. Make sure the person you are promoting:

- can administer billing, or at least has billing access on the account
- is someone you would trust with `data.wipe`
- will actually be reachable — if they are on holiday, nobody can do these things until they are back

## Running the transfer

Go to **Settings → Organization** and use the transfer control. Choose the new owner and confirm.

The change is immediate. The previous owner is demoted to **admin** — not removed. They keep full access to the workspace, they just no longer hold billing authority.

## What does not carry over

The new owner does not inherit your personal session. They sign in as themselves, with their own account, and the role is attached to their membership row.

## If the owner leaves the company

Transfer before they go. A transfer needs the current owner's session, so once the owner is gone the organization is stuck at that role until support intervenes.

This is the single most common way organizations get stranded. If someone is departing, transfer ownership in the same sitting as removing their access.
