# Support tickets

How to get help, and what to expect after you ask.

## File a ticket

1. Go to **Support → Tickets → New**.
2. Pick a **category**: General, Bug report, Billing, Feature request, Account, or Other.
3. Pick a **priority**: Low, Normal, High, or Urgent.
4. Write the message. What you were doing, what happened, and what you expected.

## Choose the priority honestly

- **Urgent** — the product is down or you are losing money. Production only.
- **High** — something important is broken and there is no workaround.
- **Normal** — something is wrong or missing, work continues around it.
- **Low** — a question, a nit, a nice-to-have.

Urgent does not make a vague ticket faster. A clear Normal ticket gets answered before a vague Urgent one every time.

## What happens next

The ticket appears in your list with its status. You get a notification when the team replies. Open the ticket to read the thread and reply — everything stays in one place, not scattered across emails.

## Before you file

Two checks that resolve most tickets faster than any reply:

- **Search the docs** — most how-answering questions are already covered.
- **Check the status page** — if something is broken for everyone, it is probably already being worked on.

## Bug reports that get fixed

The tickets that get fixed fast have three things:

1. **The exact steps** — "I clicked X, then Y" beats "it does not work".
2. **The exact error** — the message, the screen, a screenshot.
3. **The context** — which agent, which channel, which organization, when it happened.
