# Creating your account

Signup takes an email address and a password. No card, no trial countdown.

## Sign up

Go to the home page and choose **Get Started**. You can register with email and password, or continue with Google.

## Verify your email

We send a verification link to the address you registered with. Until you verify, some settings stay read-only and outbound channels cannot be enabled. The link expires, so request a new one from the login screen if it lapses.

## Signing in afterwards

Use the same email and password, or Google if you registered that way. Sessions persist across devices, and you can review and revoke active sessions from your profile settings.

## If you get stuck

Password resets are self-serve — request a link on the login screen and follow it from the same browser you requested it in. If email never arrives, check spam and confirm your provider is not blocking the sending domain.
