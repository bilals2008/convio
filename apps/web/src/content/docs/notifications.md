# Notifications

How to stay on top of what your agents are doing without living in the dashboard.

## The notification center

The bell in the top bar opens your notifications: new conversations, ticket replies, billing events, deployment failures, and system events. Unread count shows on the bell.

Open one to read it. Mark it read, or archive it once it is handled — the list is yours to clear.

## Set your preferences

**Settings → Notification preferences** controls what reaches you and when.

**Categories** — pick which event types you want notified about. If a category is noise for your role, turn it off; the events are still in the center.

**Quiet hours** — set a start and end time and notifications go silent overnight. The events are not lost — they stack up in the center for the morning.

## What actually needs you

Most notifications do not need a reaction. The ones that do:

- **Deployment failed** — a channel stopped answering. Check credentials first; that is the usual cause.
- **Ticket replied** — someone is waiting on you.
- **Billing threshold** — the plan allowance is running out. See [Billing and usage](/docs/billing).

Everything else is awareness. Let the unread count be a backlog, not an alert stream.
