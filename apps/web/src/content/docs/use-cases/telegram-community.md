# Telegram community assistant

Deploy an AI agent to your Telegram group or channel to answer common questions, share resources, and keep your community informed — 24/7.

## What you build

A Telegram bot that lives in your group and answers member questions about your product, service, or community guidelines.

## Setup

1. **Create a Telegram bot** — message @BotFather, get your token.
2. **Create an agent** — write a prompt scoped to your community's needs.
3. **Attach a knowledge base** — upload FAQs, getting-started guides, and community rules.
4. **Deploy to Telegram** — paste your bot token in Settings → Deployments.
5. **Add to your group** — add the bot as a member or admin.

## Tips

- Use Q&A pairs for the questions new members always ask.
- Set guardrails to keep the bot on-topic in large groups.
- Use the playground to test how the bot handles off-topic or hostile messages.
- Deploy the same agent to Discord and Slack with one click — same brain, more channels.
