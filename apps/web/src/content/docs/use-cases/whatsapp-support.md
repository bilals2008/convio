# Customer support on WhatsApp

Deploy an AI agent on your WhatsApp Business number to answer customer questions instantly — order status, returns, shipping, and FAQs — without hiring a support team.

## What you build

An agent that lives on your WhatsApp Business number and handles the 80% of repetitive questions your team gets every day.

## Setup

1. **Connect WhatsApp** — link your WhatsApp Business number via Kapso in Settings → Deployments.
2. **Create an agent** — start from the FAQ template or write a support prompt.
3. **Attach your knowledge base** — upload FAQ docs, return policies, shipping info, and product specs.
4. **Add a lookup tool** — give the agent read-only access to your order system so it can check status in real time.
5. **Set active and deploy** — flip the agent to `active` and deploy to WhatsApp.

## Tips

- Add Q&A pairs for your top 20 most-asked questions — they outperform document retrieval.
- Set business hours so the agent deflects gracefully outside working hours.
- Use the playground to test edge cases before going live.
