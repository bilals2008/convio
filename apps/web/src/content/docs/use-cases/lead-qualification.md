# Lead qualification on your website

Add a web widget to your site that qualifies visitors one question at a time — budget, timeline, team size, use case — and hands warm leads to your sales team.

## What you build

A conversational widget on your pricing or contact page that replaces a static form with a guided conversation.

## Setup

1. **Create an agent** — write a prompt that asks qualifying questions one at a time.
2. **Attach a knowledge base** — upload pricing pages, case studies, and comparison docs.
3. **Create a widget** — go to Widgets → New, pick your agent, customize the theme.
4. **Add to your site** — paste the embed script before `</body>`.
5. **Set the agent active** — draft agents accept nothing.

## Tips

- One question per message — do not overwhelm visitors.
- Cap at 2 unanswered questions before offering a human handoff.
- Use quick replies to make answers effortless.
- Add your domain to the widget allowlist so it only loads on your site.
