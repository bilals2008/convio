# Internal helpdesk on Slack

Give your team an AI assistant that answers internal questions — onboarding, runbooks, architecture decisions, HR policies — right inside Slack.

## What you build

An agent deployed to your Slack workspace that anyone on the team can @mention to get instant answers from your internal docs.

## Setup

1. **Create an agent** — write a prompt scoped to internal knowledge.
2. **Attach your knowledge base** — upload onboarding docs, runbooks, architecture decision records, and team policies.
3. **Add Q&A pairs** — curate the questions new hires ask most.
4. **Deploy to Slack** — connect your workspace and add the bot to relevant channels.
5. **Set active** — flip to `active` and your team can start asking.

## Tips

- Start internal before going customer-facing — it is low-risk and builds your knowledge base.
- When someone reports a bad answer, add a Q&A pair. The agent gets better every week.
- Use the playground to test sensitive questions before the whole team sees them.
